📋 Google Sheets Player Sync
How to connect your Google Sheet:
1. Open your Google Sheet with player data.
2. Go to File → Share → Publish to web.
3. Under "Link", select your Stats sheet and choose Comma-separated values (.csv).
4. Click Publish and copy the URL.
5. Open this HTML file in a text editor, find the SHEETS_URL constant near the top of the script, and paste your URL there.
6. Save and redeploy. Player data will now auto-sync every 6 hours.
